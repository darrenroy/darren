Concepts worth knowing. Add your own notes or links as you go.

Inducement & liquidity grabs
Concept
Price sweeps a recent high or low to grab liquidity before reversing. The indicator is designed to flag these moments. Don't chase — wait for confirmation after the sweep.
Open range trading
Setup
The first 15–30 min of RTH sets the day's initial range. Breaks above OR high or below OR low with follow-through are among the cleanest intraday setups.
Risk-to-reward discipline
Fundamentals
At a minimum 2:1 R:R, you can be wrong 40% of the time and still be profitable. Sizing and stop placement matter more than finding perfect entries.
Reading market structure
Concept
Higher highs and higher lows = uptrend. Lower highs and lower lows = downtrend. Identify the structure on a higher timeframe before dropping down to find entries.
Trading psychology
Mindset
FOMO and revenge trading are the two biggest account killers. Your journal is your best tool — log everything, especially the bad trades, and look for patterns in your behavior.
